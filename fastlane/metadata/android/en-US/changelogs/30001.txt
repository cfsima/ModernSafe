ModernSafe 3.0.0 (Initial Release):
- Ground-up rewrite in 100% Kotlin with Jetpack Compose & Material 3.
- Upgraded vault security to AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2 with automatic legacy migration.
- Added Android Autofill Service support.
- Added BiometricPrompt unlock (fingerprint and face recognition).
- Dynamic theming, custom color palettes, and edge-to-edge layout.
- Added automatic locking on screen off and session timeout.
- Fully offline: zero network permissions.
