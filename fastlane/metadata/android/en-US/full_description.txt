ModernSafe is a private, offline password vault for Android, succeeding the classic OI Safe with a complete modern rewrite.

Key Features:

* Fully Offline: ModernSafe has no internet permission. Your data never touches any cloud or remote server.
* Strong Encryption: Uses AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption paired with PBKDF2 key derivation.
* Android Autofill: Fill usernames and passwords seamlessly inside apps and web browsers.
* Biometric Authentication: Unlock quickly and securely using fingerprint or face unlock.
* Material 3 Design: Clean Jetpack Compose UI with dark mode, edge-to-edge support, and custom themes.
* Auto-Lock: Protects your credentials by locking automatically when your screen turns off or after inactivity.
* Legacy Support: Seamlessly import and transparently upgrade backups from original OI Safe vaults.
* Backup & Export: Export encrypted backups or export to CSV.
